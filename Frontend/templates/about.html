Our Mission

Making academic planning simple, structured, and achievable for every student.

Why we built it

Students juggle multiple courses, assignments, projects, and personal commitments. Without a plan, it's hard to know where to start or how to stay on track. Study Planner was built to close that gap — turning what's in your head into a clear plan you can follow.

Instead of asking "What should I study today?", you'll have a concrete answer based on your goals, priorities, schedule, and progress.

What we believe

Effective studying isn't about more hours with the books — it's about a clear plan, good time management, and consistency.

Small, consistent actions lead to significant academic results.

Our Philosophy

Effective studying is built on three simple principles.

  • 1

    Plan

    Know what needs to be done and when it needs to be done.

  • 2

    Focus

    Give your attention to the right task at the right time.

  • 3

    Progress

    Track your efforts, learn from your habits, and keep improving.
